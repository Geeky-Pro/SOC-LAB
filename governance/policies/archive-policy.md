# Archive Policy

- Archive is a preservation area, not an active content workspace.
- Existing archived files must not be silently deleted or overwritten.
- Reuse requires review for accuracy, versions, safety, licensing, and fit with current learning outcomes.
- Migrated content must be recreated or adapted in its canonical destination, not edited in place in the archive.
- Record important provenance when historical material informs a released asset.
- Any bulk archive cleanup requires explicit Project Owner approval.
