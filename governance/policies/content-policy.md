# Content Policy

- Teach concepts and investigative reasoning independently of any particular product.
- Identify tool names, versions, editions, and limitations when demonstrating implementations.
- Use clear learning objectives and measurable expected outcomes.
- Separate student instructions from instructor guidance and solutions.
- Cite authoritative references for technical claims.
- Label assumptions, examples, simulated evidence, and unverified configurations.
- Avoid filler, duplicated content, and empty scaffolding.


## Bilingual content requirements

- Treat Arabic and English as first-class languages.
- Preserve equivalent learning outcomes, technical meaning, scope, procedures, evidence, safety boundaries, and assessment criteria.
- Do not translate code, commands, SPL, field names, configuration keys, protocol names, product names, or standard identifiers.
- Retain canonical English terminology with a clear Arabic explanation where useful.
- Link paired variants and disclose missing or pending translations.
- Do not claim parity based only on automated translation or matching headings.
- Follow `bilingual-content-policy.md` for detailed requirements.
