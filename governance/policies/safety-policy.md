# Lab and Content Safety Policy

- All attack simulation and testing must remain within explicitly authorized, isolated lab assets.
- Define scope, prerequisites, expected impact, cleanup, and rollback before potentially disruptive activity.
- Never direct students to test public systems or third-party infrastructure.
- Use harmless test artifacts and controlled simulations where possible.
- Do not include live credentials, real personal data, or uncontrolled malware.
- Include instructor supervision requirements for higher-risk exercises.
- Stop an exercise if it escapes the authorized scope or causes unexpected impact.
