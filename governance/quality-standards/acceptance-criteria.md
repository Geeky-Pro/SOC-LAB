# Content Acceptance Criteria

A deliverable is ready for Project Owner approval only when applicable criteria are met:

- Purpose, audience, learning outcomes, and prerequisites are clear.
- Technical statements are supported by reliable sources.
- Tool versions, assumptions, and limitations are identified.
- Practical steps are complete, safe, and reproducible in the documented scope.
- Expected evidence and outcomes are described.
- Cleanup, recovery, and troubleshooting are included where relevant.
- Student and instructor materials are correctly separated.
- Links, terminology, formatting, and file placement are consistent.
- Independent review findings are resolved or explicitly accepted.
- Validation status is honest and supported by evidence.
- Licensing and security requirements are satisfied.

Approval is a human decision and must not be inferred from an automated check or AI recommendation.
