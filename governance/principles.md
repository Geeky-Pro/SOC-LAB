# Project Principles

1. **Competency first:** organize learning around skills and security outcomes, not product names.
2. **Concept before tool:** explain the security mechanism before demonstrating a product implementation.
3. **Evidence-led investigation:** learners must support conclusions with observable telemetry and reasoning.
4. **Reproducibility:** document prerequisites, versions, configuration, expected results, cleanup, and recovery.
5. **Safety by design:** simulations remain isolated, authorized, bounded, and reversible.
6. **Progressive complexity:** build from foundations toward integrated investigations.
7. **Separation of concerns:** distinguish shared infrastructure, module content, reusable templates, governance, and historical material.
8. **Independent review:** authors do not approve their own work.
9. **Transparent validation:** distinguish review from execution and test evidence.
10. **Maintainability:** prefer concise, modular documentation over duplicated or empty scaffolding.
11. **Source traceability:** record authoritative references and relevant version/date context.
12. **Human accountability:** the Project Owner retains final decision and release authority.
