# Security Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to the SOC-LAB repository, its documentation, scripts, lab configurations, and teaching scenarios.

## Reporting a concern

Do not publish credentials, exploitable secrets, student personal information, or sensitive operational details in public issues. Report security concerns privately to the repository owner through an appropriate private channel.

## Lab safety

- Execute offensive simulations only in explicitly authorized, isolated lab networks.
- Never target public systems, third-party infrastructure, or networks outside the exercise scope.
- Document impact, prerequisites, cleanup, and recovery for disruptive actions.
- Keep secrets out of source control and use placeholders in examples.
- Review scripts and configurations before execution.

## Supported content

Security findings in instructional content should include enough context to reproduce and verify them safely, without exposing real credentials or private data.
